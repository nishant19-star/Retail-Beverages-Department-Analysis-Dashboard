# Beverages Department Analysis | Power BI Dashboard
A two-page Power BI dashboard that analyzes the performance of the Beverages
department over a rolling 12 months (May 2025 – May 2026).

## Project Objective
Help business teams understand how the department is performing, which regions
and categories drive sales, and where growth opportunities exist.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Department Health
- KPI cards: Sales, % Sales Contribution, Transactions, Customers,
  % Customer Penetration, Quantity Sold, Avg Transaction Value (ATV),
  Avg Selling Price, Units Per Trip, Spend Per Customer
- Regional sales distribution (North, South, East, West, Central)
- Month-on-month Sales vs ATV trend

### 2. Category Performance
- Pareto analysis: which categories drive 80% of sales
- Category scorecard with YoY sales, transaction and ATV growth
- Category mix and growth across store formats (Supermarket, Express, Fresh)
- Discount impact on monthly category sales
- Monthly sales and MoM growth by store format

## Key Insights
- Beverages generated **₹179.51M** in sales (**20.0%** of total contribution)
  from **544K transactions** and **14,147 customers**.
- **North** is the top region at **26.0%** of department sales.
- **Tea & Coffee** contributes **60.02%** of current-year sales; together with
  Health Drinks & Supplements, it makes up about 80%.
- **Soft Drinks & Juices** shows the strongest YoY growth (**36.5%**).
- ATV declined YoY across categories while transaction counts grew,
  suggesting growth is volume-led.
